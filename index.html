ABOUT THIS APP

Simple books for your business

Track money coming into and going out of your small business. The dashboard shows your overall totals and latest activity, while Transactions lets you search and filter your full record.

Add a description, amount, type, and category for each transaction. Your records are saved in this browser, so they will still be here when you return on the same device and browser.

Built with: HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, and browser local storage.

Amounts shown in Kenyan shillings (KES)
